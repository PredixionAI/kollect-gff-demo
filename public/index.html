Every collections floor still runs on people dialing lists.

A missed EMI becomes a ticket. The ticket becomes a call that goes unanswered, then a scripted reminder the borrower learns to ignore. By the time a human reaches them, the account has aged and the relationship has cooled.

The problem was never the borrower. It was that one strategy was never built to fit every borrower.

It calls the borrower, in their language, at a time they will pick up.

It listens, and negotiates a plan the borrower can actually keep.

It sends the plan on WhatsApp before the call ends.

Then it tells your team exactly what happened, and what to do next.
